==============================================================================
WORLD LIFECYCLE
==============================================================================

The World Lifecycle defines how a Voxel World is created, used and destroyed.

Every world follows the same lifecycle regardless of project type.

==============================================================================

CREATION

A new world begins in an empty state.

The Voxel Grid is initialized.

Internal managers are created.

No chunks are allocated initially.

==============================================================================

INITIALIZATION

During initialization the world prepares:

Chunk Management.

Palette.

Selection System.

History System.

Dirty Tracking.

Internal caches.

The world is then ready for editing.

==============================================================================

EDITING

During editing:

Chunks may be allocated.

Chunks may be destroyed.

Voxel data changes.

History grows.

Selections evolve.

Dirty Chunks accumulate.

==============================================================================

SAVE

Saving serializes the authoritative voxel data.

Temporary caches are excluded.

Meshes are excluded.

Rendering data is excluded.

Only persistent world data is written.

==============================================================================

LOAD

Loading reconstructs the world from serialized data.

The world becomes the new authoritative state.

Derived structures are rebuilt automatically.

==============================================================================

IMPORT

Importing inserts new voxel data into an existing world.

Imported data follows the same validation rules as manual editing.

Import operations generate History when appropriate.

==============================================================================

EXPORT

Exporting never modifies the Voxel World.

Exporters operate in read-only mode.

==============================================================================

CLEAR

Clearing removes every chunk.

History may optionally be cleared.

Selections are reset.

Dirty state is reset.

==============================================================================

SHUTDOWN

Before destruction:

Pending operations complete.

Temporary memory is released.

Caches are discarded.

Internal references are destroyed.

Persistent data must already have been saved if required.

==============================================================================

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

The World Lifecycle guarantees predictable behavior throughout the entire
lifetime of a Voxel World.

==============================================================================
