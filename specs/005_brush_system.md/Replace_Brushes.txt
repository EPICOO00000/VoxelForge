==============================================================================
REPLACE BRUSHES
==============================================================================

Replace Brushes perform conditional voxel transformations.

==============================================================================

PURPOSE

Replacement tools automate repetitive editing tasks.

==============================================================================

MATCH CONDITIONS

Replace operations may match:

Block Type.

Material.

Metadata.

Tags.

Height.

Orientation.

Custom Filters.

==============================================================================

REPLACEMENT MODES

Replace All.

Replace First Match.

Replace Random.

Replace Pattern.

Replace Weighted.

==============================================================================

SEARCH AREA

Replacement is limited by:

Brush Shape.

Selection.

Chunk.

Entire World.

==============================================================================

RULES

Only matching voxels are modified.

Non-matching voxels remain unchanged.

==============================================================================

MULTI-REPLACE

One source block may map to multiple destination blocks.

Weighted probabilities may be used.

==============================================================================

PREVIEW

Replace Brushes should preview affected voxels before execution.

==============================================================================

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Replace operations should enable fast large-scale editing while maintaining
deterministic results.

==============================================================================

END OF REPLACE BRUSHES
