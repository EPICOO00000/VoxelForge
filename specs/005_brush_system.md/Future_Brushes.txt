==============================================================================
FUTURE BRUSHES
==============================================================================

This document defines the long-term evolution of the Brush System.

These features are intentionally outside the initial implementation.

==============================================================================

PURPOSE

The Brush architecture should remain flexible enough to integrate future
editing technologies without redesigning the engine.

==============================================================================

NODE-BASED BRUSHES

Future versions may allow Brushes to be built using visual node graphs.

Nodes may represent:

Shapes.

Behaviors.

Filters.

Noise.

Conditions.

Outputs.

==============================================================================

LAYERED BRUSHES

Multiple Brushes may execute together as one composite Brush.

==============================================================================

BOOLEAN BRUSHES

Brushes may support Boolean operations:

Union.

Difference.

Intersection.

==============================================================================

SIGNED DISTANCE FIELDS

Future Shapes may be generated from Signed Distance Fields.

==============================================================================

PHYSICS BRUSHES

Possible future simulations include:

Gravity.

Collapse.

Sand.

Liquids.

Snow.

==============================================================================

AI BRUSHES

Machine Learning may assist:

Terrain generation.

Shape completion.

Material suggestions.

Automatic detailing.

AI must remain optional.

==============================================================================

SCRIPTABLE BRUSHES

Users may create Brushes through scripting APIs.

==============================================================================

NETWORK BRUSHES

Collaborative editing may synchronize Brush execution across multiple users.

==============================================================================

ANIMATED BRUSHES

Brush parameters may evolve over time.

==============================================================================

PROCEDURAL STACKS

Multiple procedural generators may execute sequentially.

==============================================================================

CUSTOM EXECUTION MODIFIERS

Future systems may inject execution modifiers without modifying the Brush
Engine.

==============================================================================

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

The Brush architecture should remain future-proof for many years while
requiring minimal changes to the existing engine.

==============================================================================

END OF FUTURE BRUSHES
