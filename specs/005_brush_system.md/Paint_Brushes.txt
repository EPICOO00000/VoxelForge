==============================================================================
PAINT BRUSHES
==============================================================================

Paint Brushes modify voxel attributes without altering geometry.

==============================================================================

PURPOSE

Painting allows artists to assign block identities, colors and metadata while
preserving voxel positions.

==============================================================================

CORE BRUSHES

Paint.

Replace.

Flood Fill.

Gradient Paint.

Random Paint.

Pattern Paint.

Material Blend.

==============================================================================

PAINT

Assigns one block type.

==============================================================================

REPLACE

Replaces only matching blocks.

==============================================================================

FLOOD FILL

Fills connected regions.

==============================================================================

GRADIENT PAINT

Interpolates between multiple materials.

==============================================================================

RANDOM PAINT

Distributes blocks using weighted probabilities.

==============================================================================

PATTERN PAINT

Applies predefined repeating patterns.

==============================================================================

MATERIAL BLEND

Transitions smoothly between compatible block types.

==============================================================================

FILTERS

Painting may filter by:

Current Block.

Height.

Slope.

Selection.

Metadata.

==============================================================================

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Painting should remain precise, predictable and non-destructive to geometry.

==============================================================================
