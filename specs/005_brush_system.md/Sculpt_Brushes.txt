==============================================================================
SCULPT BRUSHES
==============================================================================

Sculpt Brushes modify the geometry of the voxel world.

Unlike Paint Brushes, Sculpt Brushes primarily affect voxel occupancy rather
than block identity.

==============================================================================

PURPOSE

Sculpt Brushes allow intuitive terrain and shape creation while preserving
predictable editing behavior.

==============================================================================

CORE BRUSHES

The engine should provide the following sculpting tools:

Raise

Lower

Smooth

Flatten

Inflate

Deflate

Scrape

Pinch

Sharpen

Relax

==============================================================================

RAISE

Adds material inside the Brush influence.

==============================================================================

LOWER

Removes material inside the Brush influence.

==============================================================================

SMOOTH

Reduces abrupt voxel transitions.

Smooth should preserve the global shape.

==============================================================================

FLATTEN

Moves voxels toward a common plane.

The target plane may depend on the initial surface.

==============================================================================

INFLATE

Expands geometry outward.

==============================================================================

DEFLATE

Contracts geometry inward.

==============================================================================

SCRAPE

Cuts elevated regions while preserving lower areas.

==============================================================================

PINCH

Pulls surrounding voxels toward the Brush center.

==============================================================================

SHARPEN

Increases local contrast.

==============================================================================

RELAX

Reduces small irregularities without significant volume loss.

==============================================================================

COMMON PARAMETERS

Radius.

Strength.

Falloff.

Symmetry.

Spacing.

==============================================================================

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Sculpt Brushes should provide intuitive terrain editing while remaining fully
deterministic.
