How generated-block replacement works
  1. Choose a generator part, such as terrain surface, tree leaves, or castle walls.
  2. Choose what should appear wherever that part is generated: its original block, one replacement block, or a palette.
  3. For a palette, choose the blocks and a noise pattern that decides which palette block is placed at each coordinate.

Example: Terrain surface → Palette + noise → Grass, Moss, Dirt. The mountain shape stays the same; only its generated surface blocks vary.
